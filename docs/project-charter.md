## Purpose

Learning project for reproducible, scientifically documented 3D-printing
experiments and parametric part development (FDM/FFF primary; SLA, SLS,
MJF, DMLS/SLM, binder jetting optional — see
`docs/reference/project-specification-de.md` §3).

## Goals

- Build FDM/FFF printing and DfAM skill through a progression of
  exercises and DoE experiments (see `CLAUDE.md` "Exercises vs. experiments").
- Produce traceable manufacturing packages for own printers and external
  manufacturing in Europe/China.

## Non-goals

- Safety-critical, medical, pressure-bearing, lifting, fire-safety,
  road-legal, aviation, electrical-safety or human-protection parts
  (see `CLAUDE.md` "Safety and scope").
